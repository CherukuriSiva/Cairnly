<Body>
Most final-year students spread themselves too thin. This roadmap is ruthlessly scoped: DSA to a passing bar, one strong project, behavioral prep, and company-targeted practice. That combination outperforms generic 'do everything' prep every time.
</Body>

<RoadmapPhase title="Months 1–2: DSA Core Patterns" duration="8 weeks">
- Focus on high-frequency patterns: arrays/maps, sliding window, two pointers, binary search, trees/graphs, DP basics.
- 2–3 problems per pattern — enough to internalize the shape, not to inflate a counter.
- Prioritize understanding over the number of problems solved. A pattern you can explain beats fifty you half-remember.
</RoadmapPhase>

<RoadmapPhase title="Months 3–4: Project + System Design" duration="8 weeks">
- Polish your best project until it is demo-able and explainable end-to-end.
- Learn system design basics: the questions interviewers actually ask, not every pattern in a textbook.
- Understand enough AI/RAG to speak to it in interviews — you don't need to build it if you can explain it clearly.
</RoadmapPhase>

<RoadmapPhase title="Month 5: Interview Mode" duration="4 weeks">
- Company-specific research: know the role, the stack, and the interview format for each target.
- Mock interviews: both coding and behavioral, with feedback you act on.
- Resume finalized and LinkedIn updated to match the roles you're chasing.
- Referral requests sent — early, politely, and to the right people.
</RoadmapPhase>

<RoadmapPhase title="Month 6: Active Applications" duration="4 weeks">
- Applications out to your targeted list.
- Pattern practice continues at lower intensity to stay sharp.
- Track status: where each application stands and what comes next.
- Interview debriefs after every round — write down what was asked and what you'd fix.
</RoadmapPhase>
