## The 6 fields that drive recruiter search results

### 1. Headline (120 characters)

This is the single highest-impact field. It appears in search results, connection requests, and comments. Do not waste it on "Aspiring Software Engineer."

**Formula:** `[Role you want] | [Top 2-3 skills] | [College] '[Grad year]`

**Examples:**
- `SDE Intern @ Razorpay | React, Node.js, PostgreSQL | IIIT-H '27`
- `Backend Engineer | Python, FastAPI, Redis | Building AI retrieval systems | VIT '27`
- `CS Undergrad | Full-Stack + LLM Engineering | NIT Trichy '28`

### 2. About section (first 3 lines are everything)

LinkedIn shows only the first 3 lines before "see more." Front-load:

```
I build full-stack web apps and AI pipelines. Currently an SDE intern
at [Company] working on [specific thing]. Graduating from [College]
in [Year] — looking for full-time SDE roles starting [Month].

Projects:
• [Project 1]: [one-line description] — github.com/you/repo
• [Project 2]: [one-line description] — github.com/you/repo

Tech I work with daily: Python, TypeScript, React, FastAPI, PostgreSQL
```

### 3. Experience section

Add internships, freelance work, open-source contributions, and teaching/TA roles. Each entry needs:
- Company/org name (LinkedIn auto-links recognized companies)
- Specific title (not just "Intern" — use "Backend Engineering Intern")
- 2-3 bullet points with outcomes, not duties

### 4. Education

Add your college, degree, CGPA (if above 8.0), and relevant coursework only if it's unusual (e.g., "Graduate-level NLP" as an undergrad). Add clubs/positions only if they show leadership.

### 5. Skills section (recruiter filter)

LinkedIn recruiters filter by skills. Add your actual technical skills — the ones on your resume. Get endorsed for your top 3 by classmates. Skill endorsements affect search ranking more than most people realize.

**Add these (if truthful):** Python, JavaScript, React.js, Node.js, SQL, Git, Docker, REST APIs, Machine Learning

**Do not add:** "Problem Solving," "Team Player," "Quick Learner" — these are not searchable technical skills.

### 6. Featured section

Pin 2-3 items:
- A link to your best GitHub project
- A link to a deployed project or demo video
- A blog post or technical write-up (if you have one)

## What NOT to do on LinkedIn

- **Do not post "Day X of #100DaysOfCode"** — recruiters do not read these
- **Do not use "Open to Work" banner** — mixed signal; use the private "Open to Work" setting instead
- **Do not list 15 certifications from free courses** — one or two meaningful ones (AWS, GCP, or a respected MOOC) are fine
- **Do not write in third person** — "Rahul is a passionate developer" reads as bizarre
- **Do not add 500+ random connections** — connection count is irrelevant; relevant connections matter

## The 20-minute LinkedIn overhaul

1. Rewrite your headline using the formula above (2 min)
2. Rewrite the first 3 lines of your About section (5 min)
3. Add/update 2-3 Experience entries with bullet points (5 min)
4. Add 10 relevant technical skills (3 min)
5. Pin 2 items in Featured (3 min)
6. Set a professional photo (2 min)
