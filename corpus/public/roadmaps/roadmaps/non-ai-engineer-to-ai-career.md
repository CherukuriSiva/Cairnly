Most non-ML engineers overestimate the theory gap and underestimate the engineering gap. Companies building with LLMs need engineers who can ship reliable AI features — not researchers. Your existing engineering skills are the asset; you just need to add the AI layer.

## Months 1–3: Foundations (12 weeks)
- Python if you're not already fluent — it's the lingua franca of the AI layer.
- ML/DL basics: what training is and how inference works — not the math.
- LLM APIs and prompting: get comfortable calling models and shaping their output.
- A basic RAG pipeline: chunk, embed, retrieve, answer.

## Months 4–6: Applied AI (12 weeks)
- Build a RAG application that solves a real problem, not a toy demo.
- Evaluation basics: measure whether your answers are grounded and useful.
- Agents with tool use: let the model call functions and act, not just chat.
- FastAPI for AI backends: a clean Python service to host your AI layer.
- One deployed project live on the internet.

## Months 7–8: Portfolio + Interview Prep (8 weeks)
- Polish 2 AI projects until they're demo-ready.
- Write a case study for each: problem, approach, trade-offs, results.
- AI engineering interview prep: RAG design questions, agent architecture, evaluation strategy.
- Update your resume to lead with AI work and your engineering track record.

## Month 9: Job Search (4 weeks)
- Apply to AI engineering roles — not ML research positions.
- Leverage your existing engineering experience as a differentiator, not a liability.
- Target companies building with LLMs, not companies building LLMs.

## Skill priorities

**Now**

- Python + LLM APIs
- RAG pipeline end-to-end
- Building real AI features (not just tutorials)
- Evaluation of AI output quality

**Soon**

- Agent patterns + tool use
- FastAPI + deployment
- AI interview prep

**Later**

- Fine-tuning
- Deep ML theory
- Research papers (unless targeting research-adjacent roles)

**Ignore for now**

- ML research without application
- Trying to match ML PhD backgrounds
- Waiting until you feel "ready"
- Courses and certificates instead of building
